( 02 ) — Our Story

More ThanJust A Cafe

The Sister's Cafe is a warm and welcoming place near the Landing Site in Bir Billing, bringing together freshly prepared food, authentic flavours and a peaceful atmosphere.

Whether you're travelling through Bir, spending time with friends or simply looking for a quiet meal, it's a place to slow down and enjoy the moment.

( 05 ) — The Menu

Made fresh. Served with love.

Ask the sisters what's cooking today — the menu changes with the season and the mood of the hills.

ICE TEA

  • Lemon Ice Tea

    Classic black tea, fresh lemon, served over ice.

  • Peach Ice Tea

    Smooth peach notes over chilled brewed tea.

  • Green Apple Ice Tea

    Crisp, tart and refreshing on a mountain afternoon.

MOJITO — MANY FLAVOURS

  • Mint Mojito

  • Lemon Mojito
